# Monster reactions

Source: [Monster Sound Effects Pack — Ogrebane](https://opengameart.org/content/monster-sound-effects-pack), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Original download: `https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/monster_sfx_pack.zip`, retained in `source/ogrebane-pack.zip`.

`tools/build-monster-voices.py` produces nine fantasy creature styles with base/attack/hurt/death reactions. Each output's original ZIP member and pitch/rate are listed in `monster-banks.json`. Mono conversion, silence trimming, filtering or modulation, normalization, fades, and limited echo create stylized game reactions. These are transformed monster performances, not authentic recordings of the named animals.

The audition download `source/starninjas-pack.zip` is [16 Monster Growls — StarNinjas](https://opengameart.org/content/16-monster-growls), CC0 1.0; retained for source comparison and not used in the shipped runtime.
