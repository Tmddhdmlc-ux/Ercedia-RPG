All 70 registered skills have explicit recipes in web/skill-audio-profiles.js. They reuse and layer suitable external samples; these are not 70 uniquely recorded or downloaded sounds. Timing follows the real VFX charge and impact phases. Layer counts are presentation only and never assign combat hit counts or damage. Browser gesture, mute and volume rules apply. Runtime decoding sets safe duration limits and fade envelopes.
