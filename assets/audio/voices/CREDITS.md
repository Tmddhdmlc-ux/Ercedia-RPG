CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Originals 01-laughter.wav and 01-anime gasp.wav, filenames renamed only.

Male attack, hurt and jump: Brandon Song (wolfwoot), Voice Clip Pack - Male Adventurer RPG. https://opengameart.org/content/voice-clip-pack-male-adventurer-rpg . CC0 1.0. Originals attack0.wav, hurt0.wav and jump0.wav, filenames renamed only.

These five files are auditions for voice tone, not a complete 18-emotion bank, newly generated voices or Korean dialogue. Male effort samples are not labeled as definitive anger, sadness or romance performances. The game does not automatically attach these samples to NPCs. voice-plan.json and voice-banks.json describe the current four-bank auditions. No performer voice cloning is requested.

## Four-bank auditions

Added Mature Female/noises from cicifyre's same CC0 Voice Clip Packs release. All selected source WAVs are kept in source/. Male extra effort/victory/gameover takes are from the same Brandon Song CC0 pack. Female-young smile and surprise are byte-identical copies of the approved bright references. Other female-young cues are selected from Type 1 (cutesy-style) nonverbal takes in cicifyre Female RPG Voice Starter Pack, https://opengameart.org/content/female-rpg-voice-starter-pack , CC0 1.0. Their original pitch is retained; trims, mono conversion, level and fades apply. Source names healed1/2/3, attack3/2, damaged1/3 are recorded in the manifest. They are not repitched Mature Female takes; female-mature uses those takes at original pitch with trims and envelopes. Male-young and male-mature are processed variants of the same performer, not different actors or new age-specific recordings. Male emotion labels, especially affectionate/embarrassed cues, are audition interpretations of effort/breath samples and require listening review. No Korean spoken dialogue is promised.

36 playable cues and exact source/modification metadata are in voice-banks.json. tools/build-voice-variants.py reproduces them. Exported processed cues are mono 16-bit 44.1 kHz with trimmed silence, a maximum of 1.5 seconds and fades; the two preserved female references retain their original length and WAV encoding. bank IDs are female_young, female_mature, male_young, male_mature. NPC default serin=female_young is registration for future attachment; the game does not yet auto-play these auditions.
